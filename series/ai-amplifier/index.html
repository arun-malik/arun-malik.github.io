AI as Human Amplifier vs Replacement

An overview and eight essays on when AI improves human work, when automation makes sense, and how to test the difference. Amplification is a design objective to evaluate, not a guaranteed result.

Complete · 9 of 9 published
  1. 00

    AI changes the work. Who gets the gains?

    Automating a task can help a worker, replace their work, or create demand elsewhere. The outcome depends on how jobs and institutions change.

    Published · Series date: 2026-07-01
  2. 01

    The Amplification Thesis

    AI can improve human work without making human-plus-AI the best option. Design for amplification, then test it against the alternatives.

    Published · Series date: 2026-07-12
  3. 02

    The Centaur Advantage

    Human-AI routing only helps if the router can identify a useful difference in ability. A chess experiment shows both the opportunity and the difficulty.

    Published · Series date: 2026-07-23
  4. 03

    The Great Equalizer

    AI can narrow performance gaps on a task. That does not mean it gives novices an expert's knowledge, pay, or independence.

    Published · Series date: 2026-08-03
  5. 04

    The Cognitive Offloading Trap

    Better assisted work and better learning are different outcomes. A classroom experiment shows why the distinction matters.

    Published · Series date: 2026-08-14
  6. 05

    Intelligence Amplification in Code

    AI coding tools can accelerate a bounded task and slow experienced maintainers. Measure the work you actually need to finish.

    Published · Series date: 2026-08-24
  7. 06

    Amplifying Creativity

    AI suggestions can improve an individual story while making a collection of stories more alike. Quality and variety need separate tests.

    Published · Series date: 2026-09-04
  8. 07

    The New Skill Premium

    Job postings show which skills employers ask for, not what caused a wage premium. Build a development plan without mistaking either for a guarantee.

    Published · Series date: 2026-09-15
  9. 08

    The Amplification Playbook

    A proposed trial for redesigning one workflow around AI, with explicit baselines, worker input, learning checks, and a decision to stop.

    Published · Series date: 2026-09-26
