Progressive Crystallization

Ten essays on agent-led, hybrid, and deterministic playbooks: where knowledge comes from, what to test, and where the behavior belongs.

A useful playbook can come from an investigation or from an expert who already understands the scenario. It can remain agent-led, combine fixed steps with AI decisions, or run without runtime AI. These are choices, not a maturity ladder. Keeping the playbook and incorporating understood behavior into service code are separate options.

The articles distinguish observations from specifications, permissions from reasoning, and reduced inference from total cost. Determinism guarantees neither correctness nor safety. Service integration need not remove a root cause. Unknown inputs call for a stop or reviewed handoff, not a silent expansion of authority.

Find the piece you need

  • Execution forms and scope: distinguish agent-led, hybrid, and deterministic work.
  • Capture worksheet: record knowledge from investigation or expert authorship.
  • Playbook contract and decision inventory: define promises, authority, and remaining judgment.
  • Runnable fixtures: inspect and test the original local status rule.
  • Recovery runbook: handle denied access, changed evidence, and incomplete work.
  • Measurement protocol and location decision record: evaluate the change and choose an owner.
  • Complete worked example: select among defensible designs without a mandatory endpoint.
