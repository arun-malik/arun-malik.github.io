A CORE MAP, FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES

AI starts before the chat box.

Start with a bowl of fruit and a notebook of orders. Follow how examples become a model, how a model generates words, and how an application turns those words into bounded, checkable work.

Sixteen complete chapters. 168 linked concepts. Read in order, or use the concept map to find a definition and the ideas it builds on.

This is a finite foundational path, not every branch of machine learning or AI. It covers data and learning, neural networks, language and multimodal models, retrieval, tools, agents, harnesses, evaluations, and operation. Advanced mathematics, robotics, and specialist research topics sit outside this first path.

No setup. No paid account. No required labs. Each chapter has an optional small observation with an answer. The cafe and all numerical examples are fictional.
